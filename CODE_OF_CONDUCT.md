# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participation should be professional, constructive, and focused on the work. Treat contributors with respect, disagree with ideas rather than people, and avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Project discussions

Technical criticism is welcome when it is specific and evidence-based. Security-sensitive information and private operational data should not be posted publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

This project-specific code of conduct is intentionally concise. It may be replaced by a recognized community code of conduct before a broader public launch.
